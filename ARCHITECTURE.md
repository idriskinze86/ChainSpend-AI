---

## Components

### Smart Contract
Stores wallet-specific expenses securely on Arc.

### AI Decision Engine
Analyzes spending behavior and generates intelligent financial recommendations.

### Dashboard
Displays expenses, summaries, AI insights, and future autonomous actions.

### Future Agent
The long-term goal is an AI agent capable of executing user-approved programmable USDC payments based on configurable rules.
