# 🤖 ChainSpend AI Prompt Library

## Expense Analysis

Analyse the user's spending and provide a concise summary highlighting the largest spending category, unusual expenses, and one practical saving recommendation.

---

## Budget Assistant

Given the user's expenses and budget limits, identify categories that are close to exceeding the budget and recommend adjustments.

---

## Spending Insights

Compare this month's expenses with previous spending patterns and explain the most significant changes.

---

## Fraud Detection

Identify transactions that are significantly higher than the user's normal spending behaviour and flag them for review.

---

## Financial Coach

Provide one actionable recommendation that helps the user improve their financial habits without restricting necessary spending.

---

## Autonomous Payment Decision

Before approving an automatic USDC payment, verify:

- Spending limits
- Available balance
- User-defined payment rules
- Scheduled payment date

Only approve the payment if every condition is satisfied.
